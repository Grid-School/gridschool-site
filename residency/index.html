Two phases. Build first, then search with me beside you.

Twice a week, we build.

Two calls a week. We pick the project your target role needs, scope it to something you can finish, and build it together: architecture, the hard parts, tests, the write-up. You drive; I'm the senior engineer beside you.

Straight answers

How is this different from Complete?

Complete gives you the course, the system and the first week's work prepared, and you run it. In the Residency I'm on calls with you twice a week for eight weeks, building your projects with you, then weekly on your search until you sign an offer.

What if I don't have a job after 12 months?

The Residency runs up to 12 months from your start. If you're still searching then, we look at what happened together and decide what's worth doing next. You keep your map and everything we built.

Can a parent pay?

Yes, as a gift. The Residency is yours, though: you accept it from your own email, you're the one on the calls, and you choose whether they get a weekly progress note (never what we talk about).

What do I need to start?

You can already write software, you have about ten hours a week, and you want a specific kind of role. If you're not sure, take the free check first; it shows where you stand.
