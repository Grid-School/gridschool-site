# Humans around the queue

*Short reading for `cap.change`. Not a node. Linked from the change lesson.*

T-1842 is a routing bug and a people fact at the same time.

Someone filed a free-text note. Someone else's queue received it. Someone else should have owned it. Each of those people has a different picture of what "done" means: filed, acknowledged, fixed, refunded, closed. If you invent a process without asking, you will optimize the code for a definition of done that nobody who owns the queue would sign.

Before the engineering contract:

1. Name who filed it, who received it, who should own it.
2. Write one sentence for what that owner would call done.
3. If you cannot name them, you do not yet know what the change is for. Ask before inventing process.

Vision here is not taste in diffs. It is choosing the change that repairs the actual failure, in language a person who owns the queue could accept.
