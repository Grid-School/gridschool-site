    I'm Aden. I build applied AI systems for engineering teams.

    Straight answers

    Will you get me a job?

    I make you the candidate a team can evaluate and trust: reviewed work on real systems, a public case study, an outside engineer's verdict, and my reference. I prepare every interview from your own work and keep working with you until you land. A company's yes is still theirs.

    I already have years of experience. Is this too junior for me?

    Your map starts where you are. For working engineers it usually skips the practice codebase and goes straight to turning past work into public proof, a project aimed at the role you want, and rehearsing the rounds where you stall.

    How is the map decided?

    The sentence at the top of this page drafts it from three answers, using the same steps the platform runs. On the call we finish it from your resume, your target companies, and where your last interviews stopped. Every step ends in something a stranger can open.

    Why not follow the map on my own?

    You can, and the draft is yours to keep. What a list cannot do is read your work and send it back, change the plan when an interview goes sideways, or sign a verdict as someone who did not help you. Most steps on your map end in a written review from me, and the defense is judged by an outside engineer. That part is what you pay for.

    Is this a bootcamp?

    No. A bootcamp teaches a stack to beginners. This starts from software you can already write and works on what gets you hired next, planned around you and reviewed every week.

    Why trust you, with no placements yet?

    Don't take it on trust. The free check runs the method on your own situation before you pay anything, and the first two course modules are yours to use. Complete has a 14-day refund on the one-time price, the monthly plan cancels any time, and the Residency has the week-2 exit.

    Can a parent pay for it?

    Yes: give it as a gift. They choose to accept it from their own email (if they don't, you're refunded in full), and if they agree you get a short weekly note on their progress.

    Can I cancel the monthly plan?

    Any time, online, in two clicks. It starts with 7 free days, then $97 a month, and it stops on its own after 12 payments. We email you 3 days before the first charge.
