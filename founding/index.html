The founding cohort · starts January 4, 2027

Sixteen weeks to your first engineering role, with a small cohort and your coach.

For final-year students and recent grads who can build something, can work in the US, and want to do the work. Eight seats.

You're in. Thank you.

Your receipt is in your email. Before January 4 I'll set up your map, send your sign-in, and book a short kickoff call. Reply to the receipt any time; it comes straight to me.

What happens each week

  • Every morning: roles that fit you, the person to reach at each, and a note written for you. You send it in your own words.
  • Once your resume is ready: 5 sends a week, 3 at the least, every reply tracked and every silence followed up.
  • A real-user project with a design partner: a conversation, a spec, the checks, something shipped, and what changed for them.
  • Clinics with your coach, and people at the same stage.
  • About 10 to 15 hours a week.

What you leave with

A resume that's ready for your titles, a record of every send and reply, a case study a hiring manager can check in ten minutes, and a 90-second walkthrough of it. Everything in GridSchool Complete is included for 12 months.

The price

$3,000, or 3 payments of $1,100. A $300 reservation holds a seat and counts toward the price.

The terms

  • The $300 reservation is fully refundable until December 28.
  • The week-2 exit: if it's the wrong fit by the end of week 2, for you or for us, the unused part comes back and later payments are cancelled.
  • A pause of up to 4 weeks, once, if life gets in the way.
  • Refunds go to whoever paid. If a parent pays, the graduate still decides whether to take part.
  • Nobody honest can promise a job. A company's yes is theirs. We publish our numbers, the weak weeks included.

The full terms are on the terms page before anyone pays.

How to join

Start with the free check, then a short fit step. If it's a fit, we talk, with the graduate on the call. Reservations open December 1.
