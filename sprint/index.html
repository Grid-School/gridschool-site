Straight answers

Why is it free?

Because we publish the numbers. At weeks 3 and 6 we share the sprint's sends, reply rate, screens and interviews, as counts across everyone. That's how the next people decide whether this works. Your name, your messages and the companies you contacted are never published.

Who is it for?

Final-year students and graduates up to about two years out of a degree or a bootcamp, who can build something and can work in the US without sponsorship. You need to have built at least one thing someone can open, like a project, a repo or a demo.

What if I already have a job?

That's fine. Most of the work fits in evenings and on weekends, Send Day can be done on your own time, and the clinics have an async option.

Is anything automated on LinkedIn?

Never. We prepare the roles, the person and the note. You read it, change it, and send it yourself from your own accounts. Nothing is ever sent for you.

What happens after six weeks?

You can join the paid founding cohort, keep going on GridSchool Complete at $97 a month, or stay on your free map. Your map and your progress stay yours whatever you choose.

What if I go quiet?

Life happens. If your seat is quiet for 7 days it goes to the next person on the waitlist, after two emails. Your map stays yours, free, and you can rejoin the waitlist when you're ready.

Is a job guaranteed?

No, and be wary of anyone who says it is. A company's yes is theirs. What we promise is the work: your roles each morning, the notes, Send Day, the clinics, and a reply from your coach within a business day.
